# Community code of conduct

Kiln welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Help make technical discussion clear, useful, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and explain tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy, consent, accessibility needs, and contributors' time.
- Credit others' work and follow applicable licenses.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions and move disagreements back to the technical question.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberately exposing secrets or private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. This applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's private **Report content** controls where available. For conduct involving a security exposure, follow [SECURITY](SECURITY.md). Do not repost private or abusive material publicly in order to report it. No separate private project contact is currently published.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or block accounts to protect the community. Responses should consider severity, context, and repeated behavior. Maintainers may ask for clarification and may keep reports confidential where practical; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed on third-party platforms.

Contributors are not expected to tolerate abuse while a report is reviewed. GitHub's own reporting and safety mechanisms remain available independently of project moderation.
